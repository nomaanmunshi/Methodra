# Content and copyright policy

Version 1 contains four book-inspired collections: Deep Work, Psycho-Cybernetics, The Pragmatic Programmer, and Make It Stick.

Rules for maintainers:

- attribute the inspiration;
- use original Methodra explanations and protocol language;
- do not reproduce modern book passages, chapter summaries, cover art, diagrams or proprietary exercises;
- do not scrape quote sites;
- do not fabricate quotations;
- keep the collection at Level C/D unless a separate component has its own cited evidence;
- cite primary research for empirical claims when possible rather than treating a popular book as the experiment.

The names of books/authors are used for factual attribution. They do not imply endorsement of Methodra.
